# Landing page structure

Use these sections in this order. Drop a section only if there is nothing honest to put in it.

1. **Hero** – headline (outcome), subheadline (how), primary CTA button, one supporting visual. Optional micro-line under the CTA that removes risk: "Free. No card needed."
2. **Problem** – 2–3 sentences in the visitor's own words describing the pain. Make them nod.
3. **Benefits** – 3 cards. Each: short title + one sentence. Outcome-first.
4. **How it works** – 3 numbered steps. Makes the offer feel easy.
5. **Proof** – real testimonials, numbers, logos or press. If none exist, replace with a "Built by" / founder note — never fake it.
6. **Offer / pricing** – what they get, what it costs, what happens next.
7. **FAQ** – 4–6 real objections (price, time, trust, "is this for me?").
8. **Final CTA** – restate the outcome + the same button.
9. **Footer** – contact, privacy link, small print.

## Headline formulas

- Get [outcome] without [pain]
- [Outcome] in [timeframe]
- The [category] for [specific audience]
- Stop [pain]. Start [outcome].

## CTA wording

Use first person and value: "Start my free lesson" beats "Submit". Avoid "Learn more" as the primary CTA.
